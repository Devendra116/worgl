    What is this?

    This is a budget that expires, not an investment.

    In 1932 the Austrian town of Wörgl issued stamp-scrip: notes that lost value unless you paid for a stamp. People spent them instead of hoarding them. Wörgl (WORGL) rebuilds that idea on NEAR.

    • There are exactly 1,000,000 coins, ever. Nothing is minted.
    • Every balance melts at one fixed rate. The demo rate is 1% per 10 minutes; the real setting is 1% per 30 days. Sending coins does not avoid the melt.
    • What melts goes to the treasury (the contract's own account).
    • The treasury pays out through a one-time faucet claim (100 WORGL per account) and payouts voted by a DAO council. Together they can spend at most 10% of the treasury per UTC day.
    • Your balance is not stored as a number that changes. The contract stores your shares; the balance is worked out from the clock whenever it is read. This page does the same sum locally, so it ticks without sending any transaction.

    Not sybil-resistant. Accounts are free, so one person can make many accounts and claim the faucet many times. Treat the faucet and any votes as a demo, not as one-person-one-vote.
